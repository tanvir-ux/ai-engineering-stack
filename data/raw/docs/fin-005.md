# Corporate card policy

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel regularly, with a monthly limit of 5,000 euros. Cards must not be used for personal expenses. Card statements are reconciled in the expense tool by the 5th of every month. A lost card must be blocked immediately through the bank hotline and reported to the finance team. Repeated policy breaches lead to the card being withdrawn.
