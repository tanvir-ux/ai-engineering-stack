# Password and MFA policy

Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts, and hardware security keys are required for administrators. Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule but must be changed immediately after a suspected compromise. Password managers approved by IT may be used to store credentials.
