# VPN and network access

Access to internal systems from outside the office requires the company VPN. The VPN session expires after 12 hours and needs a new multi-factor prompt. Split tunnelling is disabled. Guest Wi-Fi in offices is separated from the corporate network and is limited to internet access. Network changes are made only by the network team after an approved change request.
