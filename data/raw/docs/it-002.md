# Laptop and device policy

Company laptops are replaced every 36 months. Disk encryption is enforced on all devices. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 1 hour so it can be wiped remotely. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app. Employees must not install software that is not on the approved list without a software request.
