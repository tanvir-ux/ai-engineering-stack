# Shipment damage claims

Damage to a shipment must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos and the delivery note. Claims up to 2,500 euros can be settled by the customer service team. Claims above 2,500 euros need approval from the claims manager. The standard carrier liability is 8.33 special drawing rights per kilogram for road freight. Customers are told the outcome of a claim within 15 business days.
