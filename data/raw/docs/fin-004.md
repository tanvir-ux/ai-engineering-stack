# Quarterly close calendar

The accounting close finishes on the 5th business day after the end of each quarter. Accrual entries must be posted by the 2nd business day. Intercompany balances are reconciled on the 3rd business day. Late journal entries after the 4th business day need written approval from the financial controller. The CFO signs off the quarterly results on the 5th business day.
