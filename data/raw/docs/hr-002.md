# Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of parental leave on full pay. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks on full pay. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date. Employees can add up to 8 weeks of unpaid leave if they tell their manager at least 8 weeks before the planned return date. Health insurance continues during all leave.
