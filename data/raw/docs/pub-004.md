# Code of conduct

All Meridian employees and contractors must act honestly, respect colleagues and follow the law. Gifts worth more than 50 euros from suppliers must be declared to the compliance team. Conflicts of interest must be reported in writing to a manager. Concerns can be raised without fear of retaliation through the ethics hotline, which is open 24 hours a day and is run by an independent provider.
