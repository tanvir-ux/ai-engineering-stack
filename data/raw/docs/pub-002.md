# Sustainability commitments

Meridian Freight commits to reach net-zero emissions by 2040. By 2028, 35 percent of the delivery fleet will be electric. All new warehouses are built with rooftop solar panels. Emissions are reported every year in the sustainability report, and the figures are audited by an external firm. Customers can request a per-shipment emissions statement from their account manager.
