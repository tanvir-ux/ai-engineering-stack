# Software request process

To get new software, open a ticket in the IT service desk with the product name, the business reason and the cost centre. IT reviews each request within 3 business days. Open source tools are checked for licence and security risk before approval. Software that costs more than 5,000 euros per year follows the procurement approval thresholds. Approved software is installed through the self-service catalogue.
