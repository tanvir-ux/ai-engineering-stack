# Customs and export compliance

Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice and a correct customs tariff code. Shipments to sanctioned countries are blocked by the booking system and cannot be released without approval from the compliance team. Export records are kept for 10 years. Dangerous goods need a declaration and a trained handler. Questions about tariff codes go to the customs desk.
