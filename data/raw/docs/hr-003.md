# Remote work policy

Employees in office-based roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone, and everyone should be reachable then. A one-time home office stipend of 400 euros is available after the probation period. Remote work from another country is limited to 20 days per calendar year and must be approved by HR because of tax and insurance rules.
