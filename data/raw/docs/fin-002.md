# Travel policy

Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy. Hotels are capped at 220 euros per night, and 280 euros in London, New York and Singapore. Trips should be booked at least 14 days in advance through the company travel tool. Train travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Travel insurance is included for all employees and covers medical costs and cancellations.
