# Access review procedure

SECURITY INTERNAL. Access to production systems is reviewed every quarter. System owners confirm each user's access within 14 days of the review starting. Access that is not confirmed is removed automatically after 21 days. Leavers lose all access on their last working day, and privileged accounts are disabled within 4 hours of the termination being recorded in the HR system.
