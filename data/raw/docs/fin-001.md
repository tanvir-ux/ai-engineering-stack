# Expense policy

Employees can claim meals up to 60 euros per day while travelling for work. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 euros. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, and approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Mileage for personal cars is paid at 0.58 euros per kilometre. Claims above 1,000 euros need a second approval from the department head.
