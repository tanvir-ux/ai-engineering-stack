# Company overview

Meridian Freight Co. is a fictional logistics company used as sample data for this project. It was founded in 2009 and has its headquarters in Rotterdam. The company employs about 3,400 people and operates 41 warehouses in 12 countries. Meridian moves general cargo, refrigerated goods and e-commerce parcels. In the last financial year it delivered 18 million shipments. The company is organised into four divisions: Road, Ocean, Air and Contract Logistics.
