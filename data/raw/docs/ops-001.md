# Warehouse safety rules

Everyone on a warehouse floor must wear a high-visibility vest, safety shoes and a helmet in loading zones. Forklift drivers need a certificate that is renewed every 3 years. Forklifts must not exceed 10 km/h inside buildings. Near misses must be recorded in the safety app on the same shift. Each warehouse holds a fire drill twice a year.
