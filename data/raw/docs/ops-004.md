# Fleet maintenance schedule

Trucks have a safety inspection every 10,000 kilometres or every 3 months, whichever comes first. Tyres are checked weekly by the driver. Electric vans have their battery health checked every 6 months. A vehicle with a failed brake test is taken off the road immediately. Maintenance records are kept for 7 years.
