# Performance review process

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and in December. Each employee is rated on a 5-point scale, where 3 means meets expectations. Managers submit ratings by the 10th of the review month, and a calibration meeting then checks that ratings are consistent across teams. An employee who receives a rating of 1 or 2 is placed on a 90-day improvement plan agreed with HR.
