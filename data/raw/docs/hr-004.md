# Compensation bands

CONFIDENTIAL. Salary bands for the current year: Level 3 is 62,000 to 78,000 euros, Level 4 is 76,000 to 95,000 euros and Level 5 is 92,000 to 118,000 euros. Salary reviews happen once a year in April, with a typical merit budget of 3.5 percent. Annual bonuses range from 5 percent of salary at Level 3 to 12 percent at Level 5.
