# Incident response runbook

SECURITY INTERNAL. A security incident is classified as Sev 1 (confirmed data breach or ransomware), Sev 2 (suspected breach) or Sev 3 (policy violation). For Sev 1 the on-call security lead must be paged within 15 minutes and the crisis team is assembled within 1 hour. Regulators must be notified within 72 hours of confirming a personal data breach. Affected systems are isolated first, and evidence is preserved before any system is rebuilt.
