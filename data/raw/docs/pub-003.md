# Customer privacy notice

Meridian Freight keeps customer shipment records for 24 months after delivery. Delivery photos are deleted after 90 days. Customers can ask for a copy of their personal data or ask for deletion by writing to the privacy office, which answers within 30 days. Meridian does not sell personal data. Shipment data is shared only with carriers and customs authorities that are needed to complete the delivery.
