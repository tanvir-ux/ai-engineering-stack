# Annual leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year, in addition to 11 public holidays. Leave should be requested at least two weeks in advance and needs the approval of the line manager. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and they expire on 31 March. Leave for part-time employees is calculated in proportion to their working hours. Unused leave is paid out only when employment ends.
