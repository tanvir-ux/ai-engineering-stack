# Procurement approval thresholds

Purchases up to 5,000 euros need the approval of a manager. Purchases from 5,000 to 50,000 euros need a director. Purchases above 50,000 euros need the CFO. For any purchase above 10,000 euros at least three written quotes are required unless procurement approves a single-source exception. Contracts longer than 12 months must be reviewed by legal before signature.
