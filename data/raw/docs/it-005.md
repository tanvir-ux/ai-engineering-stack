# IT support service levels

The IT service desk uses four priorities. Priority 1, a major outage, gets a first response within 15 minutes, any time of day. Priority 2 gets a response within 1 hour. Priority 3 gets a response within 1 business day. Priority 4 gets a response within 3 business days. Tickets are numbered in the form INC-1234. Users can see the status of their own tickets through the service desk tool.
